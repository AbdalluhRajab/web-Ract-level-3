# 2–5 Minute Demo Script

1. Start the app and show the public landing/products area.
2. Demonstrate login and the protected dashboard.
3. Show dashboard KPIs, sales/orders and low-stock information.
4. Open Products and demonstrate search, filtering, sorting, pagination and CRUD.
5. Show Customers, Categories and Orders.
6. Demonstrate a notification and the theme/settings controls.
7. Open the challenge/demo area and briefly show advanced features such as the reusable table, modal, error boundary and local-storage hook.
8. Explain the architecture: pages, components, contexts, hooks, services, reducers and utilities.
9. Mention the three major technical decisions and known limitations.
